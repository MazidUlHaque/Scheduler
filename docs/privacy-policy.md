# Privacy Policy - Kokhon

*Last updated: 6 November 2026*

Kokhon ("the app") is made by Md. Mazid-Ul-Haque. This policy explains what the app does with your information.
In short: **your plans stay on your phone.** Kokhon has no account, no server and no advertising, and it collects nothing.

## What the app stores

- **Your plans**: names, dates, times, places, people, notes, reminders and their history.
- **The message or screenshot text a plan was made from**, so you can see where it came from.
- **Your settings**: alarm behaviour, presets, discreet mode, daily summary.

All of this is stored **only on your phone**, in the app's private storage. Android may include it in your own
device backup (for example Google backup) if you have turned that on; that backup belongs to your Google account
and is governed by Google's policies.

## What the app reads, and when

- **Text you paste or share** with Kokhon, to find plans in it.
- **The clipboard**: only when you tap "Make it a plan" or "Paste". The app checks whether text was copied without
  reading it, so it never reads your clipboard in the background.
- **Screenshots and photos** you pick, share or take for Kokhon. They are read **on your phone** with Google ML Kit's
  on-device text recognition. Images are never uploaded or kept by Kokhon.
- **New screenshots** (only if you turn on "Offer plans from new screenshots"): when you take a screenshot, the app
  reads it on your phone and shows a suggestion if it contains a plan. It needs access to your photos for this,
  which you can withdraw at any time in Android settings.

## What the app sends

Nothing. Kokhon does not send your data anywhere, contains no analytics or advertising, and needs no internet
connection for any feature.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Notifications | To show reminders and the daily summary |
| Alarms & reminders (exact alarms) | To ring at the exact time you set |
| Full-screen notifications | To show the alarm screen over the lock screen |
| Run at startup | To set your reminders again after the phone restarts |
| Vibrate, foreground service | To vibrate and keep an alarm ringing until you stop it or it times out |
| Photos (optional) | Only for "Offer plans from new screenshots" |

## Children

Kokhon is a general-audience app and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
