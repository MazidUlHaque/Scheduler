## App name (30 characters max)
Kokhon - Know When

## Short description (80 characters max)
Turn chat messages and screenshots into plans with calm, reliable reminders.

## Full description

Plans are made in chats, not in calendars. Kokhon (কখন, "when?") turns them into reminders in seconds.

**Paste it, share it or screenshot it**
Copy "let's try to sit for a meeting tomorrow around 9:30 pm" and Kokhon makes a Meeting tomorrow at about
9:30 PM. Share messages from any app, or pick a chat screenshot: the text is read on your phone.

**See what it understood**
The words Kokhon understood are highlighted like a highlighter pen. Tap any of them to fix it. If "9:30" could be
morning or evening, it asks.

**Plans change? So can yours**
Share "let's make it 10 pm instead" and Kokhon finds the plan and asks: "Move Team meeting to 10:00 PM?"

**Reminders that fit the plan**
Choose how important a plan is and get a matching set of reminders: one quiet notification, or 30, 15 and
5 minutes before with the last one ringing. A dinner date reminds you to get ready and when to leave; a trip
reminds you to pack the day before.

**Alarms that never embarrass you**
A big Stop button, a separate Snooze, "I'm aware - no more reminders", a ring limit, and a volume press to
silence. Discreet mode makes everything vibrate only.

**And more**
- Month calendar, search and a "Next up" widget
- Morning summary of your day
- Clash and duplicate warnings
- Understands Bangla and Banglish ("kal rat 9.30 e meeting")
- No account, no ads, no internet needed. Your plans never leave your phone.
